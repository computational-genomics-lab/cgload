About this portal

This is the web view of a cgload database. cgload loads a genome assembly and its annotation into SQLite or PostgreSQL in one transaction, compares what it stored with an independent count of the input file, and rolls back a load that falls short. Every assembly shown here passed that check when it was loaded; its page shows the load's receipt.

What you can do

  • Search a genome by gene name, synonym, locus tag, protein or transcript ID, a word of a product, a cross-reference such as GeneID:672, or a functional term such as GO:0005524, K00799 or PF00069.
  • Open a gene to see its transcripts drawn to scale, its attributes and functional annotation, and its genomic, spliced, coding and protein sequences.
  • Browse any region in the genome browser (igv.js), which reads the assembly and the annotation files that cgload wrote at load.
  • Download a gene's family as GFF3, sequences as FASTA, or the whole assembly and annotation.

For programs

Every page has a JSON counterpart under /api/v1/: /api/v1/genomes, /api/v1/genomes/<id>/search?q=, /api/v1/genomes/<id>/features/<row> and /api/v1/genomes/<id>/region?r=.

What it does not do

The portal changes nothing: it opens the database read-only, has no accounts and takes no uploads. Protein sequences are translated by cgload from the CDS rows, with the genetic code the file names; where a file declares an exception that a plain translation does not apply (transl_except, ribosomal slippage), the page says so.
